Privacy

Customer privacy.

HStudio uses the details you provide, such as your name, email address and mobile number, to create and manage appointments and customer accounts.

Booking information is stored securely using our booking system provider. We only use customer details for providing HStudio services and appointment communications. We do not sell customer information.

You can ask HStudio to correct or delete customer information where applicable by contacting the studio on 07561 885972.

Payment processing and messaging providers may process information under their own privacy terms when those services are enabled.
