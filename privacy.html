Fictional environment.

North & Co. is an interactive sales demonstration, not a real barber shop. Names, customers, appointments, contact details and business information shown in the demo are fictional.

Please do not enter real personal, business or payment information while exploring the demonstration.

The demo uses a live booking backend so the customer journey and management dashboard can demonstrate how the system behaves in practice.
