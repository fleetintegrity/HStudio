North & Co. is a fictional barber shop created to demonstrate a customer booking journey and barber-shop management system.

Appointments created here are demonstration records only. No haircut or real-world service is being purchased or reserved.

The three-hour cancellation rule, service prices, payment options and operational information shown in the interface exist to demonstrate configurable booking-system features.

Please use fictional information when testing forms and do not submit real payment details.
