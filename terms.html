Booking terms

Appointments & cancellations.

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 3 hours before the booked start time. Changes inside that period may not be accepted.

Please arrive in time for your appointment. If you are significantly late, HStudio may need to shorten or rearrange the appointment to avoid delaying other customers.

Prices shown when booking are the standard service prices. Where online payment is offered, any applicable payment or refund information will be shown during the booking process.

For help with an appointment, contact HStudio on 07561 885972.
